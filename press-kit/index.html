Short bio

ROBBIE P is an Australian performing and recording artist, and the creator of Bitcoin Slang. He rhymes in the language of sound money so you never forget it.

He has been writing raps for more than 20 years, came up through Melbourne battle rap, and first sold his music for bitcoin in 2013. Bitcoin Slang (2019) turned the Bitcoin glossary into bars. Since then: OFF THE FLOOR, released on streaming and as an NFT album; yearly Bitcoin Pizza Day singles; the 2026 album DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE; and CHESTER, recorded at FBT Studios in Detroit. Stages include Bitcoin Alive in Sydney, Camp Nakamoto in New Hampshire, and El Salvador during the Plan B Forum. He releases through Jeremy Legal Records.
