The arc

Twenty years of bars

2002

First home demo as a teenager. Scarface, Geto Boys, Big Pun and Eminem: the way they played with words.

2013

Selling his music for bitcoin, years before it was cool to rap about it. It was also Gzutek’s official debut EP, Get The Flame.

2019

Bitcoin Slang: he hit the glossary and turned every term into a bar. The 3-minute orange pill.

2022

OFF THE FLOOR on streaming, then minted as an NFT album. A web3 experiment.

2024–26

Stages and streams on three continents: Bitcoin Alive in Sydney, Camp Nakamoto in the US, then El Salvador for the Max & Stacy Invitational and the Plan B Forum.
