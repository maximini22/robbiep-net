Story

From the red dirt

Who he is

Street-level poetry, aristocratic satire

“I take the social, political, and financial zeitgeist of our time and turn it into sharp, unflinching stories that push uncomfortable truths into awareness.”

An Australian performing and recording artist who moves between sound money, finance and raw personal reflection. Not from the hood: from the red dirt, the studio and the stage.

“In rap people always rap about money and I wanted to change that… if I'm going to talk about money it's going to be about Bitcoin.”

In his words

The arc

2002

A teenager with a home demo

Techno and piano lessons first, now a multi-instrumentalist on guitar, bass and uke. Scarface, Geto Boys and Eminem taught him “the way they're playing with words.”

2013

Debut studio release

Gzutek: Get The Flame, his official debut EP, sold for bitcoin years before it was cool to rap about it.

Nov 2019

Bitcoin Slang

A parody of a 90s New York rapper's street slang, flipped into the Bitcoin lexicon. “No one ever did it for an online community.”

2022

OFF THE FLOOR

“Part album, part test. I released it as NFTs to see if fans could own the music directly, and learned plenty along the way.”

2024–2026

Three continents

Bitcoin Alive in Sydney, Camp Nakamoto in New Hampshire, then El Salvador for the Max & Stacy Invitational and the Plan B Forum. Then DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE, FREEDOM and CHESTER.

Advice to his younger self

“Trust everything I thought I was doing was right. Back myself.”

The Carbon Life Pods, 2023

Satire as armour

Meet Jeremy Legal

A satirical music solicitor, written and played by Robbie, who fronts Jeremy Legal Records. “Rappers and comedians can still have this creative license to say the extreme stuff.”

He chose self-authored characters over being cast: “If I wanted to pursue acting I would basically be told what to do and how to do it, and it's far from how I operate.”
