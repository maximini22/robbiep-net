DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE

Discography  /  DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE

Album, 13 tracks, recorded primarily in Western Australia.

DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE is an album made to be played front to back. It moves between satire, signal and speculation, pulling from finance, technology, identity and myth, and each track feels like a dispatch from a different timeline.

Take Money Off Mainstreet and In The Vault boil modern finance down to simple moments. Most Humble Fiat Minimalist and Fliponomics hold ideology up as a mirror, Jetpack and Rich In Bitcoin play with ambition and excess, and Don't Shake The Snowglobe and Circular Economy Scifi World are mood pieces rather than predictions.

It was recorded primarily in Western Australia with a tight circle of collaborators, and the sound stays clean and uncluttered. Australian artists Jwittit and Sixpence feature alongside Man Like Kweks, CoinIcarus of Pleb Underground appears in a news report, and production is by MixedbyJD, Notion, Young Zee, YKLBLZ, Shapeshifter404 and Robbie P.
