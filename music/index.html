Off The Floor · 2022

The NFT album

OFF THE FLOOR was Robbie P's experiment with NFTs as a Bitcoin artist. The day after it hit streaming (4 Mar 2022) it was minted as an NFT album: 80 standard editions and 20 Nuclear Deluxe, with five bonus tracks for holders only until they reached streaming on 20 Apr 2022. Looking back, Robbie put it simply:

“I did my own little NFT thing but I didn't like that I was doing it on Ethereum. I wanted Bitcoin.”BTC Sessions, 2023
