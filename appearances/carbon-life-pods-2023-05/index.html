Talking Bitcoin Slang with Coach Carbon

Appearances  /  The Carbon Life Pods · season 2

The backstory

An early one. Josias “Coach” Carbon came across Bitcoin Slang while he was doubling down on his audio podcast and invited Robbie on The Carbon Life Pods. They recorded on 21 Apr 2023 and the episode went out on 17 May 2023.

They talked about music, precious metals, getting past the lockdowns and helping people get over their doubts about Bitcoin. Coach Carbon used Robbie's advice to his younger self as the episode's pull quote.

In his words
“My whole thing these days is to do cool things and document it… the story behind something is more interesting most of the time.”The Carbon Life Pods, 2023
“By doing Bitcoin music I'm dialing in directly into a hyper niche, and from there I'm able to build an audience of people that like hip hop.”The Carbon Life Pods, 2023
“Trust everything I thought I was doing was right. Trust it 100%. Back myself.”The Carbon Life Pods, 2023
