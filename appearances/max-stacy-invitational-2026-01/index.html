Live with JV at the Max & Stacy Invitational

Appearances  /  BTC News Alerts · Bitcoin Country Live, Max & Stacy Invitational

The backstory

At the Max & Stacy Invitational in El Salvador, Robbie finally met JV of BTC News Alerts, who streamed live for about five hours on main day.

Robbie joined the stream several times: an opening interview, an a cappella verse from “In The Vault”, the first play of “Take Money Off Mainstreet”, and a long stretch co-hosting with other guests. He talked about Bitcoin Slang and the coming album DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE, out a week later.

In his words
“It's educational, it's fun. You can, I guess, learn about money while you're listening to music.”BTC News Alerts stream, Jan 2026
“Originally I'm from Melbourne, right? So in the last 12 months, I moved across to Perth. I recorded an entire album in Perth, Western Australia.”BTC News Alerts stream, Jan 2026
“I'm using myself as a case study for others to follow… I'm building out the record label. I'm trying to do this sovereign artist thing.”BTC News Alerts stream, Jan 2026
