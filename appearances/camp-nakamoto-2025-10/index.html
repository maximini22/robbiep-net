First US show at Camp Nakamoto

Appearances  /  Camp Nakamoto · Lake Winnipesaukee, New Hampshire

The backstory

“Camp Nakamoto was such a cool event and very unique to the other conferences I've been at.” The island Bitcoin retreat on Lake Winnipesaukee, New Hampshire, ran from 10 to 13 Oct 2025, and it gave Robbie his first performance on US soil.

From there he went on to Detroit and recorded at FBT Studios, the sessions that became CHESTER, before heading to El Salvador in January 2026.

In his words
“Camp Nakamoto was such a cool event… Got a chance to perform for the first time on USA soil.”robbiep.net
“After performing at CAMP NAKAMOTO in New Hampshire, I headed to Detroit and recorded at the legendary FBT Studios, the same rooms where Jeff Bass and his team shaped early Eminem and D12.”CHESTER Bandcamp release notes, 2026
“I was on the way down here to El Salvador just after traveling for four months through Canada, America. I stayed in Detroit working some music.”Orange Pill Studios, Plan B 2026
