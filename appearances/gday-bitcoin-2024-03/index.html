Fireside with Sean Clarke

Appearances  /  G'Day Bitcoin · Sean Clarke, at Bitcoin Alive 2024

The backstory

During Bitcoin Alive 2024 in Sydney, Robbie and Sean Clarke of G'Day Bitcoin sat down for an impromptu interview podcast, recorded on the day of his headline set. Sean posted it on 5 May 2024 as “Defining Bitcoin Culture w/ Rhyme & Rap”.

Robbie opened with a verse from GOING CASHLESS, then talked about writing songs since 2001, playing live by 2007, and rapping about money long before Bitcoin.

In his words
“I was stacking gold and silver before I heard of Bitcoin.”G'Day Bitcoin, 2024
“All it takes is one crusade and someone takes your gold. So it was the unconfiscatable aspect to it that really made me think, hang on, this is now next level.”G'Day Bitcoin, 2024
