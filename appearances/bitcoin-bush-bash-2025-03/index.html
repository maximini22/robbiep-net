Appearances  /  Beechworth Bitcoin Bush Bash · Beechworth, Victoria

The backstory

The Bitcoin Bush Bash is a free, Bitcoin-only weekend with no tickets and no KYC. The 2025 edition ran from 28 to 30 March in Beechworth, in north-east Victoria.

Two days out, the organisers announced on Nostr that “Robbie P, the Most Toxic Bitcoin Maxi” would join them for a “special 'bit-hop' performance”. He played Bitcoin Slang at Beechworth Town Hall and posted the clip on 19 Sep 2025, with Camp Nakamoto next on the list.
