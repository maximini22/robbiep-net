Appearances  /  Satoshi's Journal · song premiere

The backstory

Satoshi's Journal, the team behind the Bitcoin Beats show, premiered MOST TOXIC BITCOIN MAXI on its site in May 2023. The song is part of his yearly Bitcoin Pizza Day drops.

The Bitcoin Beats show notes still point listeners to the premiere page, but that page now returns 404, so the exact premiere date and text can't be checked.
