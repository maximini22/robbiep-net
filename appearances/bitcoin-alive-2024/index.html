Headline set at Bitcoin Alive

Appearances  /  Bitcoin Alive · Sydney

The backstory

“This was one of those epic moments I still can't believe happened.” A headline set at Bitcoin Alive in Sydney, his first full live show in years, running Bitcoin Slang and Most Toxic Bitcoin Maxi for a room of Bitcoiners.

Just before he flew out, he caught up with Robin Seyr to talk about the music industry and how Bitcoin can fix it. During the conference he also sat down for an impromptu podcast with Sean Clarke of G'Day Bitcoin.

In his words
“My goal for this conference is to do as good of a job as I can performing and just bring energy and bring excitement and entertainment to then have other people say we want him at our conference.”Robin Seyr, before Bitcoin Alive 2024
“I want to thank you all for being here today, 'cause you're taking responsibility over your financial responsibility, the direction of your family and… the autonomy of your own sovereign self.”On stage, Bitcoin Alive 2024
“I absolutely smashed it, it was the best thing ever.”Love is Bitcoin with Dani Go, 2024
