Bitcoin fixes music, with Robin Seyr

Appearances  /  Robin Seyr · YouTube

The backstory

Robbie caught up with Robin Seyr just before he flew out to Bitcoin Alive. They spoke about the state of the music industry and how Bitcoin can fix it.

They covered slow streaming royalties, Lightning zaps on Wavlake, owning his own rights without a label, and the earlier name, Gzutek.

In his words
“Not having to rely on receiving my royalties after 9 months of a stream, like I can get zapped… instantly on Wavlake.”Robin Seyr, 2024
“I'm not appreciated in that environment as much as I am in the Bitcoin ecosystem… that's where those doors are really opening up for me.”Robin Seyr, 2024
“Something that an AI can't replace. An AI won't be able to replace me. That's my goal.”Robin Seyr, 2024
