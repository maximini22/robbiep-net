Back at Bitcoin Alive

Appearances  /  Bitcoin Alive 2025 · The Roundhouse, UNSW, Sydney

The backstory

Three days before the event, Robbie posted that Bitcoin Alive had asked him back “for another round on stage at the Roundhouse”, with the classics and some unreleased music from the upcoming album. Bitcoin Alive 2025 ran on 8 Mar 2025 at the UNSW Roundhouse in Sydney.

Two weeks later he posted the Showtime Sketch, a one-minute skit filmed at the event by the Cass Brothers: moments before showtime, Robbie and Jeremy Legal hit a snag. No full recording of the 2025 set has turned up yet.
