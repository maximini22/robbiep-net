On air with Paulfactor at 3CR

Appearances  /  3CR 855AM · Melbourne community radio

The backstory

Paulfactor had Robbie in the studio at 3CR, Melbourne's 855AM community station, on 9 Oct 2023. Paulfactor introduced him by his old name, Gzutek, from the days they first met.

They went from his first home demo in 2002, through the songs ALL TIME HIGH, LESSONS OF REGRET and FOMO, to how piano lessons turned into guitar, bass and ukulele. Robbie posted the interview in three parts on his channel on 29 to 31 Jan 2025.

In his words
“When I came across the Geto Boys… Scarface… Eminem and those guys back in the early 2000s, I thought to myself, this is phenomenal, the way they're playing with words.”3CR with Paulfactor, 2023
“I've tapped into a hyper niche of people that like finance and dabbled in some hip hop, and it's a nice cross genre.”3CR with Paulfactor, 2023
“When you do it long enough you can start to basically become a multi-instrumentalist.”3CR with Paulfactor, 2023
