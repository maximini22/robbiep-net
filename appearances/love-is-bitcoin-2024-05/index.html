Can you educate through music?

Appearances  /  Love is Bitcoin · Heartbit with Dani Go

The backstory

Dani Go opened her Heartbit show with Bitcoin Slang, then sat down with Robbie for about an hour. He set the record straight on the intro: he was selling music for bitcoin in 2013 and 2014, but the first Bitcoin song came later, in 2019.

They covered the Bitcoin Alive set two months earlier, the NFT years, Lightning and AI in music, and the Most Toxic Bitcoin Maxi / Bitcoin Slang 7" vinyl he sold at the conference for bitcoin.

In his words
“I absolutely smashed it, it was the best thing ever.”Love is Bitcoin, 2024
“I think it would be more empowering if we were more willing to teach people what Bitcoin really is instead of sugar coating it.”Love is Bitcoin, 2024
