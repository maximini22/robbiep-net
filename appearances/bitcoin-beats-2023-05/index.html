Appearances  /  Bitcoin Beats · Block 2 (Satoshi's Journal)

The backstory

Bitcoin Beats is a live Bitcoin music show from Satoshi's Journal. The host found Bitcoin Slang through an uncredited clip of people putting up Bitcoin stickers, asked who made it, and ended up bringing Robbie on stage. It was 9:30 in the morning in Australia.

After playing Bitcoin Slang, they talked about how he wrote it as a parody of Big L's “Ebonics”, why Bitcoin songs can sound cheesy, and listeners' questions from the audience. The episode was published as Block 2 on 21 May 2023, 1 hour 8 minutes long.
