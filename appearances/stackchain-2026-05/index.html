The backstory

Stackchain Magazine edition #8, Meme Team 8, runs ROBBIE P as its artist feature: a full spread with an in-depth conversation, plus high-definition images from the album artwork. The cover was designed by CoinLordsHQ.

Robbie announced it on his channel on 7 May 2026 (Perth time), and pre-orders opened on 10 May 2026 through the Stackchain Magazine store at proofofink.com.
