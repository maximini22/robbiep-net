Appearances  /  Orange Pill Studios · Plan B Forum, El Salvador

The backstory

Brandon Martin of Orange Pill Studios pulled Robbie aside for a short, unplanned interview during the Plan B Forum in El Salvador in January 2026. The clip runs just under five minutes.

He had come down after four months travelling through Canada and the US, including time in Detroit working on music, and had just been at the Max & Stacy Invitational.

In his words
“Sometimes as musicians, you know, we always struggle with the output consistency because sometimes you've got to absorb and then release. And that's the role of an artist.”Orange Pill Studios, Plan B 2026
“We're here in El Salvador, banks are obsolete. This is the future. Get with it.”Orange Pill Studios, Plan B 2026
