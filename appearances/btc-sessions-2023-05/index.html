The backstory

The Most Toxic Bitcoin Maxi video caught Ben's attention at BTC Sessions, and Robbie was invited onto Bullish Bit, the live segment that ran every Saturday at the time.

The full live show, “Why are we bullish?”, also had Mary Imasuen and the Tuttle Twins on the panel. BTC Sessions later posted Robbie's answer as a short clip.

In his words
“I first sold the music for Bitcoin in 2013.”BTC Sessions Bullish Bit, 2023
“I did my own little NFT thing but I didn't like that I was doing it on Ethereum. I wanted Bitcoin.”BTC Sessions Bullish Bit, 2023
“I don't want a bank account. I just want to get paid in Bitcoin.”BTC Sessions Bullish Bit, 2023
