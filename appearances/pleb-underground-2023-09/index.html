The backstory

Catching up with Coinicarus and Walton on the Pleb Underground podcast: he dropped a verse and shared his take on the Bitcoin news of September 2023.

On the rabbit hole: he heard about Bitcoin at two dollars, and in 2013 he was selling music for Bitcoin on a centralised app called Bit Tunes. Coinicarus later appears on DREAD PIRATE ROBBIE, on “Pleb Underground Bunker”.

In his words
“In rap people always rap about money and I wanted to change that.”Pleb Underground EP54
“If I'm going to talk about money it's going to be about Bitcoin, not the USD, not AUD.”Pleb Underground EP54
“After 20 years of writing raps you want to kind of explore different ways to play with words.”Pleb Underground EP54
“The reason why I feel like I'm building sustainably with it is because… it's for me.”Pleb Underground EP54
