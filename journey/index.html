Chapter 01 · Melbourne · SYN FM 90.7

Word War 1

As Robbie tells it: back when he went by Gzutek, he went bar for bar on Word War 1, an on-air battle rap segment on SYN FM 90.7 out of RMIT in Melbourne, alongside Notion, Nicotine, 1/6 and Mo by Dash. Proof of D12 judged the final round on site.

Chapter 02 · Melbourne

Rawlab & The Burning Underground

Robbie came up with the name Rawlab himself, first as a record label name, years before Rawlab properly kicked off. Around 2010 TNI began using it for the record label Rawlab Entertainment, and the crew built on the name to keep rolling forward. His early work as a producer, including the Kawcayshan LP, was recorded and mixed at R.A.W.L.A.B HQ.

The Burning Underground, a Melbourne hip hop video magazine, came out of the same camp; T and Gzutek hosted Season 2 in 2010.

Chapter 03 · Melbourne · 2011–2016

Midnight Green Records

Recording with JD (MixedbyJD) at Stronghorn Studios, then signing to his label, Midnight Green. Get The Flame came out on 9 Jul 2013; “Hurt No More” landed on triple j Unearthed that May. “Stardust” followed in 2016, the same year he supported Rittz's Top of the Line tour in Melbourne (26 Aug 2016).

Chapter 04 · 2010–2017

Sound-money seeds

Years before the Bitcoin raps, the money questions were already in the bars: “Money Women Power Greed” (T feat. Vishus & Gzutek, on T's album The Place Where I Wannabe, 2010), “Money In The Briefcase” (a Big T bonus track produced by Gzutek, re-cut for The Silent G Chronicles), “Language Factory” from Get The Flame, and “Radical” (2017).

“accept it like fiat currency up on the system / digitz on the screen they could go missing in an instant”Radical
Chapter 05 · Detroit · 10 Dec 2025

Left to right: Jeff Bass, ROBBIE P, Jake Bass. FBT Studios, Detroit.

FBT Studios

“After performing at CAMP NAKAMOTO in New Hampshire, I headed to Detroit and recorded at the legendary FBT Studios, the same rooms where Jeff Bass and his team shaped early Eminem and D12.”ROBBIE P, Chester press release

Jake Bass now runs FBT, the studio the Bass Brothers set up in 2004. He engineered the CHESTER vocals, recorded on Eminem's old Sony C800G. FREEDOM picked up fresh guitars in the same rooms.

Chapter 06 · 2019–2026

The Bitcoin-era stages

“I go by ROBBIE P now” (Mar 2019). Bitcoin Slang dropped on 15 Nov 2019, and the stages followed: a headline set at Bitcoin Alive in Sydney (22 Mar 2024), his first US performance at Camp Nakamoto in New Hampshire (Oct 2025), then El Salvador in Jan 2026, live on the BTC News Alerts stream at the Max & Stacy Invitational and an Orange Pill Studios interview during the Plan B Forum.
